### 16.2 后端性能深度优化

#### 数据库性能优化

1.  -- 智能索引策略

2.  CREATE INDEX idx_menu_restaurant_time

3.  ON menus(restaurant_id, created_time DESC);

4.  

5.  -- 查询优化示例

6.  EXPLAIN ANALYZE

7.  SELECT m.\*, COUNT(o.id) as order_count

8.  FROM menus m

9.  LEFT JOIN orders o ON m.id = o.menu_id

10. WHERE m.restaurant_id = \$1

11. AND m.status = 'active'

12. AND m.created_time \> NOW() - INTERVAL '7 days'

13. GROUP BY m.id

14. ORDER BY order_count DESC

15. LIMIT 20;
