## 📊 监控与运维

### 应用监控配置

1.  \# Prometheus配置

2.  global:

3.  scrape_interval: 15s

4.  

5.  scrape_configs:

6.  \- job_name: 'smart-restaurant-services'

7.  metrics_path: '/actuator/prometheus'

8.  static_configs:

9.  \- targets: \['user-service:8080', 'menu-service:8080', 'inventory-service:8080'\]

10. labels:

11. group: 'backend-services'

12. 

13. \- job_name: 'frontend-services'

14. static_configs:

15. \- targets: \['web-app:80'\]

16. labels:

17. group: 'frontend-services'

18. 

19. \# Grafana仪表板配置

20. \- name: '业务监控'

21. panels:

22. \- title: '订单量监控'

23. type: 'graph'

24. targets:

25. \- expr: 'sum(rate(order_created_total\[5m\]))'

26. legendFormat: '订单创建速率'

27. 

28. \- title: '成本监控'

29. type: 'graph'

30. targets:

31. \- expr: 'cost_total'

32. legendFormat: '总成本'

33. 

34. \# 告警规则

35. groups:

36. \- name: business_alerts

37. rules:

38. \- alert: HighOrderFailureRate

39. expr: rate(order_failed_total\[5m\]) \> 0.05

40. for: 2m

41. labels:

42. severity: warning

43. annotations:

44. summary: "订单失败率过高"

45. description: "订单失败率已经超过5%，当前值为 {{ \$value }}"
